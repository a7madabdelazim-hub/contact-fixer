Contact Prefix Fixer

Adds missing country codes and removes repeated numbers

🔒 Runs only on this phone. Your contacts are never uploaded.

1. Open your contacts file

Export it first from the Contacts app:

  1. Contacts → tap < Lists (top left)
  2. Long-press All Contacts → Export
  3. Save to Files, then come back here
These are sample contacts. Open your own file to fix your real contacts.

2. After saving: put the fixed contacts back
  1. Keep your original export. It is your backup.
  2. Best way (no duplicates): on a computer open iCloud.com → Contacts → select all → Delete. Then use Import Contact and pick the all contacts file you saved here.
  3. iPhone only: save changed contacts, delete those contacts in the Contacts app (use “Copy names” for the list), then open the saved file in Files and tap Add All Contacts.
  4. Apps like Botim or WhatsApp can add their rows again the next time they sync. To stop that, turn off their Contacts access in Settings → Apps.
